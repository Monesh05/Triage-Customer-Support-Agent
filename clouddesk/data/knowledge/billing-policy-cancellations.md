# Billing Policy — Subscription Cancellations

Customers may cancel their subscription at any time from the dashboard's billing page.

## When cancellation takes effect

CloudDesk does not prorate cancellations. A cancelled subscription remains active, with full
access to its plan's features and rate limit, through the end of the current billing period
(`renewal_date`), and is not renewed after that. No partial-month credit or refund is issued
for the unused remainder of a period when a customer cancels partway through it — cancelling
is effective at period end, not immediately.

## Re-subscribing

A cancelled customer can start a new subscription (on the same or a different plan) at any
time; this creates a new subscription record rather than reactivating the old one, and
billing resumes from the new subscription's own start date.

## Cancellation is not a refund

Cancellation and refunds are governed by separate policies. Cancelling a subscription does
not, by itself, entitle a customer to a refund for time already paid for in the current
period — see the refund policy for what does qualify for a refund (e.g. duplicate charges).
